About BBMS

Connecting donors, hospitals, and patients — one drop at a time.

Our Mission

Making sure no request for blood ever goes unanswered

BBMS was built to close the gap between willing donors and hospitals in urgent need. We maintain a live registry of donors and blood stock levels, so hospitals can find the right blood group fast — and donors can see exactly where their contribution makes the biggest difference.
